# OutpostMod

OutpostMod is a modification of ZoneMod played most often on Outpost 22. To see what ZoneMod offers, please read: https://github.com/SirPlease/L4D2-Competitive-Rework/wiki/Zonemod 

OutpostMod deviates in the following ways:
- Bonus System
  - Pills are worth a static 25 points
  - The multiplier for health and distance points is 0.3
  - On a 400 point map this would look like: `R#1 Bonus: 580 <100.0%> [Health: 360 | Damage: 120 | Pills: 100]`
  - The display system is also updated to show just points instead of percentage by default, as shown above
- Weapons
  - Weapon limits are enforced to be 2 shotguns and 2 smgs
  - There is a primary weapon trading system by pressing [USE] on a survivor that is close to you, the other player must accept to trade weapons
- Boss Infected
  - The witch is now able to spawn again, with at least a 20% gap between the Tank
  - The witch can enter the saferoom and target survivors in the saferoom
  - There are no standing witches
  - It is possible to claw the witch to make her angry right away, or hold any movement key against her. It is not patched unlike EQ
- Misc
  - It's possible to bunny hop again as anything but the Tank, it is extremely difficult due to tickrate
  - Anti-baiter exists in 4v4s, 3v3s, and 2v2s, to prevent stalling across all modes
  - There are minor map modifications to typically played competitive custom maps
    - Dead By Dawn (Static witch spawn on chapter 1)
    - Diescraper (Tanks can only spawn beyond 55% on chapter 1)
  - There is several custom map configurations, usually ports to other varations of maps
    - The Dark Parish
    - The Sacrifice Dark
    - Cold Stream: Storm
    - Dead Center: Christmas Edition
